    #8680. gradually

    'grædjuəli 逐步地；渐渐地

    🎤例句

    • Jill gradually became aware of an awful smell. （吉尔渐渐地闻到了一股难闻的气味。）

    • Gradually, my ankle got better. （我的脚踝慢慢好了。）

    🤔同义词
    • stage by stage, step by step （逐步地；渐渐地）
