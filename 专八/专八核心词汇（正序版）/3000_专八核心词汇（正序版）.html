    #3000. discreet

    dis'kri:t 谨慎的；小心的

    🎤例句

    • He assured her that he would be discreet. （他向她保证会谨慎行事。）

    • I stood back at a discreet distance. （我谨慎地保持一段距离站在后面。）

    🤔同义词
    • careful, cautious, prudent, wary （谨慎的；小心的）
