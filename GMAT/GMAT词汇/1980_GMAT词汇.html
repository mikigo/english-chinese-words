    #1980. dismay

    dis'mei 沮丧，灰心；惊慌

    dis'mei 使沮丧；使惊慌

    🎤例句

    • The thought of leaving filled him with dismay . （想到要离开，他心里充满了忧伤。）

    • The committee was dismayed by what it had been told. （该委员会被所告知的情况搞得焦虑不安。）

    🤔同义词

    • depression, alarm, disappointment （沮丧，灰心；惊慌）

    • depress, dispirit （使沮丧；使惊慌）
