    #1020. depress

    di'pres 使沮丧；按下

    🎤例句

    • The thought of taking the exam again depressed him. （想到要补考，他就心情沮丧。）

    • It depresses me that nobody seems to care. （似乎没有人在乎，这让我感到很沮丧。）

    🤔同义词
    • drive down, weigh down （压低；使沮丧；使萧条）
