    #1080. owe

    əu 欠；应把…归功于

    🎤例句

    • How much do I owe you (= often used to show that you want to pay for something )? （我该付你多少钱？）

    • He owes his success to hard work. （他的成功是靠勤奋工作。）

    🤔同义词
    • appreciate, be grateful to （欠；感激；应给予；应该把……归功于）
