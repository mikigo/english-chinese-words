    🎤例句

    #6786. geochemical

    ,dʒi:ə'kemikəl 地球化学的

    🎤例句

    • But there’s very little doubt that if methane emissions are as large as this, there will be severe biological and geochemical impacts. （但有一点很肯定，如果甲烷排放量确实是如此大，必将产生严重的生物和地球化学影响。）
