## 2291. deserve

**应受， 值得**

:::note{title=🎤例句}

- I would never hit anyone, even if they deserved it. （我决不会打人，哪怕他欠揍。）

- People who are sent to prison for drunk-driving get what they deserve . （那些醉酒驾车被送进监狱的人是罪有应得的。）

:::
