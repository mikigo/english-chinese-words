## 2180. notwithstanding

**尽管**

**尽管 <**

**尽管 <**

:::note{title=🎤例句}

- His relations with colleagues, differences of opinion notwithstanding, were unfailingly friendly. （他与同事们尽管有意见分歧，但关系一直很融洽。）

:::

:::warning{title=🤔同义词}

- still, toujours （尽管，仍然）

- although, while, though, as, albeit （虽然）

:::
