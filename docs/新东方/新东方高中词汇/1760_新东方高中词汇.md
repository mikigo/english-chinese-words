- personal income tax （个人所得税）

- personal care （个人护理；个人护理用品）

- personal character （个性；个人性格）

:::

:::note{title=🎤例句}

- My personal view is that we shouldn’t offer him the job. （我个人认为我们不该给他这份工作。）
