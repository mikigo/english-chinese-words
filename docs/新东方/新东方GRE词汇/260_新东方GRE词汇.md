## 260. marvel

**对…感到惊异**

**奇迹**

:::tip{title=🤩短语}

:::

:::note{title=🎤例句}

- The whale, like the dolphin, has become a symbol of the marvels of creation. （鲸，和海豚一样，已经成为造物杰作的象征。）

:::

:::warning{title=🤔同义词}

- phenomenon, miracle, wonder （奇迹）
