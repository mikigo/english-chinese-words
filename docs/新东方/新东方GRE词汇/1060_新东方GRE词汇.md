## 1060. sun-bronzed

**被太阳晒成古铜色的**

:::note{title=🎤例句}

- Nine younger sister mother see big sister to report, and hot plates sun-bronzed the four xi. （九妹见大妹去报告母亲，又用滚烫的铜壶把“四喜”烫成了古铜色。）

:::

:::
