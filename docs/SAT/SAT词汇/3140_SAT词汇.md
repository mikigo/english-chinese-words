## 3140. interpolate

`in'tə:pəuleit`  **篡改；插入新语句**

:::note{title=🎤例句}

- He started humming a tune, then interpolated the odd word as though having difficulty remembering. （他开始哼起一个曲调，尽管记得不太清楚，他又插入了几个词。）

:::

:::warning{title=🤔同义词}

- tamper with, manipulate （篡改；插入新语句）

- build in, plug in （插入；篡改）

:::
