## 140. at last

**终于；最后**

:::note{title=🎤例句}

- The resolution was repassed at last. （最后决议还是重新通过了。）

- They cast down their weapons at last. （最后他们终于放下了武器。）

:::

:::warning{title=🤔同义词}

:::
