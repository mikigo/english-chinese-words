:::note{title=🎤例句}

- The shop’s daily profit is usually around $500. （这家商店每天的利润一般在500美元左右。）

- She sold the business and bought a farm with the profits. （她卖了公司，用所得利润买下了一座农场。）

- They sold their house at a healthy profit . （他们卖掉房子，赚了一大笔钱。）

:::
