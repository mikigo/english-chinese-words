:::

## 98. persnickety

- There's a persnickety approach to watches on the part of men that equates to a female specificity about shoes, hair color or beauty treatments. （男人对于手表的挑剔程度，与女人对于鞋子、头发颜色或美容服务的在意程度相同。）

:::

:::warning{title=🤔同义词}
