`hɔ:lt`  **停止, 暂停, 中断**

:::tip{title=🤩短语}

- grind to a halt （慢慢停止）

- The President has called for a halt to the wave of emigration. （总统呼吁遏制移民国外的热潮。）

:::

- break, stop, rest, stay, stand （停止；立定；休息）

- put a stop to （使停止；使立定）

:::
