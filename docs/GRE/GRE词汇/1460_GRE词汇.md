# 1460_GRE词汇

## 1460. avaricious

`,ævə'riʃəs`  **贪财的, 贪婪的**

:::note{title=🎤例句}

- He sacrificed his own career so that his avaricious brother could succeed. （他牺牲了自己的事业，其目的是让他贪婪的弟弟成功。）

:::

:::warning{title=🤔同义词}

- greedy, grasping （贪婪的；贪得无厌的）

:::
