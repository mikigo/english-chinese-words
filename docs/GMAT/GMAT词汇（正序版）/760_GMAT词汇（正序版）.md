## 760. denunciatory

`di'nʌnsiətəri`  **指责的；攻击的；非难的**

:::note{title=🎤例句}

- It's in this period that Milton increasingly begins to adopt, or assume, Saint Peter's confident and denunciatory rhetoric. （就是在这段时间弥尔顿越来越接受，去采用，圣彼得的有信心的责难似的语言。）

:::
