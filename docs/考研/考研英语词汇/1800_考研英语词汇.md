## 1800. awkward

`'ɔ:kwəd`  **尴尬的；笨拙的；棘手的；不合适的**

:::note{title=🎤例句}

- an awkward silence （令人尴尬的沉默）

- Philip’s remarks put her in an awkward position (=  made it difficult for her to know what to do  ) . （菲利普的话使她很窘迫。）

:::

:::warning{title=🤔同义词}

- testing, embarrassed （尴尬的；笨拙的；棘手的；不合适的）

:::
