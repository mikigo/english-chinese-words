- space suit （航天服）

- tang suit （唐装）

- following suit （跟牌）

- long suit （n. 长处；长牌）

:::

- The climate there will suit you down to the ground (= suit you very well ) . （那儿的天气你会非常满意。）

:::

:::warning{title=🤔同义词}

- become, condition, season, adapt to something （适合；使适应）

- litigation, group, team, lawsuit, law （诉讼；组；套装；恳求）
