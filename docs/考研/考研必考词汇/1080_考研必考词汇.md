- Their ideas diffused quickly across Europe. （他们的观点很快在欧洲传播开来。）

- His writing is diffuse and difficult to understand. （他写的东西冗长难懂。）

:::

## 1099. midst

`midst`  **中间,当中**

:::tip{title=🤩短语}

:::

:::
