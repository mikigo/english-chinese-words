## 358. virtually

`'və:tʃuəli`  **实际上，事实上**

:::note{title=🎤例句}

- Virtually all the children come to school by bus. （几乎所有的孩子都乘公交车上学。）

- He was virtually unknown before running for office. （他在参加竞选之前几乎不为人知。）

:::

- actually, really, almost, nearly, most （事实上，几乎；实质上）

:::
