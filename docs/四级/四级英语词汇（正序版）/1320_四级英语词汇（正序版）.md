# 1320_四级英语词汇（正序版）

## 1320. eventually

`i'ventʃuəli`  **终于；最后**

:::note{title=🎤例句}

- He eventually escaped and made his way back to England. （他终于逃脱并返回英格兰。）

- Eventually, she got a job and moved to London. （最终，她找到了工作并移居伦敦。）

:::

:::warning{title=🤔同义词}

- finally, ultimately, lastly （最后，终于）

:::
