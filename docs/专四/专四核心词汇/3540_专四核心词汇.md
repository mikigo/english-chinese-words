## 3540. frustrate

`frʌs'treit`  **挫败；阻挠；使感到灰心**

`frʌs'treit`  **挫败的；无益的**

:::note{title=🎤例句}

- Their attempts to speak to him were frustrated by the guards. （他们想跟他说话，但被警卫拦住了。）

:::

:::warning{title=🤔同义词}

- defeat, discomfit （挫败；阻挠；使感到灰心）

- founder, come to naught （失败；受挫）

- thwarted, unprofitable （挫败的；无益的）

:::
