## 3480. discard

`dis'kɑ:d, 'diskɑ:d`  **抛弃；放弃；丢弃**

`dis'kɑ:d, 'diskɑ:d`  **抛弃；被丢弃的东西或人**

:::tip{title=🤩短语}

- into the discard （废弃不用；被遗弃）

:::

:::note{title=🎤例句}

:::

- fall out of, render up, to renounce, to abandon, to give up （放弃）

- rejection, abandonment （抛弃；被丢弃的东西或人）

:::
