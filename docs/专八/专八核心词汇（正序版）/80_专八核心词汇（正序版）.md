# 80_专八核心词汇（正序版）

## 80. accomplice

`ə'kʌmplis`  **同谋者，[法] 共犯**

:::note{title=🎤例句}

- Witnesses said the gunman immediately ran to a motorcycle being ridden by an accomplice. （证人们说该枪手立即向由一名同犯驾驶的摩托车跑去。）

:::

:::warning{title=🤔同义词}

- complice, co-conspirator （同谋者，[法]共犯）

:::

## 84. accord
