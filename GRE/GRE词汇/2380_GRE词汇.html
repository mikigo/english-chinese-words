    • The hills recede into the distance and now we are in vast grasslands dotted with termite mounds, skyscraper sandcastles of the insect world. （山丘已经远去，我们现在行进在宽广的草原上，草地上有白蚁堆，那是昆虫世界的摩天大楼。）
