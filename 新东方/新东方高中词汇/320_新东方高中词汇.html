    #320. probably

    很可能， 大概

    🎤例句

    • The White House probably won't make this plan public until July. （白宫可能要到7月份才会公布这项计划。）

    • Van Gogh is probably the best-known painter in the world. （梵高可能是世界上最知名的画家。）

    🤔同义词
    • possibly, maybe, presumably （大概；或许；很可能）
